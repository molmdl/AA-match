### 1.4 SCORE-03 composition determination ("what finish must mean")

- **Finish (molecule)** = the molecule carries a recorded result — either **passed** (all required interactions formed at confirm) or **skipped** (partial score, SCORE-05 mercy path, 06-10-approved "mercy, no refusal"). A blind confirm never finishes a molecule.
- **Finish (level)** = every molecule of the level carries a recorded result (passed or skipped). Then SCORE-03's "advances to the next level with higher difficulty" fires unchanged (atomic `advance_level`, 06-03 law).
- **Give Up never finishes anything** — it ends the game at the current stage (spec.md:44; SCORE-06). It is not a per-molecule resolution.
- Consequence: SCORE-03's text needs **no rewrite**. The definition rides the SCORE-01 amendment and the ROADMAP criterion-1 NOTE (§2). If the planner prefers belt-and-suspenders, an optional parenthetical is:

```
- [x] **SCORE-03**: Finishing a level (every molecule carries a recorded result — passed or skipped; a blind confirm never finishes) advances to the next level with higher difficulty
```

Recommendation: leave SCORE-03 text untouched; the parenthetical is redundant with the SCORE-01 amendment and the ROADMAP NOTE.
