### P3 — principal-inertia "ligand-frame steps" are NOT viable. Toward-ligand already covers the useful direction.

### P3 verdict — ligand-frame steps: NOT VIABLE as the primary fix

1. **Discontinuity risk is REAL but narrow**: guanidinium's exact in-plane degeneracy (planar 3-fold symmetry, λ1 = λ2 to 4 decimals) makes its in-plane axes arbitrary — any implementation flips them 90°/44.8° under float32-level noise; 9/18 ligands sit at ratio < 1.15 (marginal). The remaining 17 are numerically stable and exactly rotation-covariant (rotcov = 0.00° for ALL 18 — a molecule and its rotated self give co-rotated axes; no within-session flip from the game's own rigid placements).
2. **The killer is perceptual, not numerical**: the ligand is static during play (front-offset once at start; the player moves only AAs), so a ligand frame = ONE fixed arbitrary basis per session. With helper visuals banned (PROJECT Key Decision: "No helper visuals during gameplay (Hint = carbon recolor only)"), its axes are INVISIBLE — the player cannot learn a frame they cannot see, and after any mouse orbit they face the same mental-rotation problem the camera frame has, now with an anchor that is neither the grid nor the view.
3. **What it would actually solve**: nothing that Toward-ligand doesn't already. "Toward the ligand is always Up" = the existing Toward-ligand button (a ligand-frame +Y step, computed from live centroids — camera-independent TODAY). The tangent directions (ligand "left/right/along plane") add keys whose directions the player cannot verify visually. The spike-axis alternative is strictly worse (51–67° swings on 3 ligands).
4. If the human ever wants ligand-frame steps anyway, the purity placement is clean (pure axis math in a new pure module or `vec3`-style helper, registered in `PURE_MODULES`; per-press reduction to `nudge_cam`-style baked world translates with identity asserts) — feasible engineering, rejected UX. PROPOSAL standing: do not build it for 8.2.
