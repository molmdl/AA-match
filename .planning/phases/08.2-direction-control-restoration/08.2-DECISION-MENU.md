## 2. Probe verdicts (numbers, not conclusions)

### P1 — The nudge math is PROVEN viewer-relative for FULL SO(3). No code fix exists.

30/30 real-wizard nudge presses across 5 scripted views (identity, the
03-04 yaw control, the ACTUAL post-start rolled play view, and 2 synthetic
full-SO(3) views) moved the picked AA exactly along the intended screen
direction (probes section 1-2, verbatim log `tmp/probe_082_p1_output.log`):

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| max press screen-direction deviation over 30 presses | **0.0000 deg** |
| max convention deviation (observed vs R^T expectation) | **2.51e-07 A** (tol 1.12e-06, the 02-15 float32 contract) |
| identity-object-matrix cleanliness after every press | **True (30/30)** |
| disorientation of the ACTUAL post-start view (screen vs world axes) | **90.0 deg** (screen-UP sits 116.6 deg from world +Y, 153.4 deg from +X; screen-RIGHT 26.6 deg from world +Y) |

**Outcome (b) of the standing re-open bullet — a code fix in
`wizard_core.view_camera_to_world` — is ELIMINATED.** The movement is
mathematically viewer-relative for every orientation. The problem is the
FRAME THE PLAYER IS GIVEN, not the math.

### P2 — Every fresh session start-composes a PURE CAMERA ROLL that buys NOTHING visible.

Every fresh session starts at R = identity and the compose applies a pure
camera roll of **+114.8 to +127.9 deg** (residual max|M-L(theta)| <=
2.5e-8 — the compose adds ONLY this roll to R; zoom preserves R exactly;
probes section 3, verbatim log `tmp/probe_082_p2_output.log`):

| Case | roll applied | roll trigger \|s\| | cam_y WITH roll | cam_y WITHOUT | FRONT cam_z (with = without) | disorientation pre->post |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 defaults seed 42 | **+116.565 deg** | 0.444 A | +0.444 A | -0.199 A | +8.24 A = +8.24 A | 0.0 deg -> **90.0 deg** |
| C2 defaults seed 4242 | **+114.775 deg** | 0.888 A | +0.888 A | -0.372 A | +8.13 A = +8.13 A | 0.0 deg -> **90.0 deg** |
| C3 defaults seed 7 | **+127.875 deg** | 0.581 A | +0.581 A | -0.357 A | +8.24 A = +8.24 A | 0.0 deg -> **90.0 deg** |
| C4 demo-easy-1 seed 42 | **+116.565 deg** | 0.444 A | +0.444 A | -0.199 A | +8.24 A = +8.24 A | 0.0 deg -> **90.0 deg** |
| C5 heme (1-mol) seed 42 | **+116.565 deg** | 0.444 A | +0.444 A | -0.199 A | +8.24 A = +8.24 A | 0.0 deg -> **90.0 deg** |

Reading (probes section 3):
- The roll's trigger offset |s| is a **0.44-0.888 A pseudo-random
  atom-population artifact** — there is no real ligand-vs-grid screen
  offset to compose.
- The roll's visible payoff is **ZERO**: post-roll cam_y = +0.44 A
  "above the grid" on a scene whose camera distance is **~97 A**
  (P1's captured view[11] = -97.2456) — a composition no human eye can
  see. Without the roll, cam_y = -0.20 to -0.37 A (a hair below — equally
  invisible).
- The front-offset delivers **100% of the visible composition**: cam_z =
  +8.1 to +8.2 A IDENTICAL with and without the roll (eyes -> ligand ->
  grid depth layering comes from `_move_ligand_in_front` alone).
- Screen axes disagree with world axes by up to **90.0 deg after compose
  vs 0.0 without** — this is the confusion source behind BOTH human
  complaints.

### P3 — Ligand-frame steps are NOT viable.

Across all 18 bundled ligands (self-checked eigen math, residuals <= 1e-9,
rotation-covariance 0.00 deg for all 18; probes section 4):

- **Guanidinium has an EXACT in-plane degeneracy** (lambda1 = lambda2 =
  1.6147, ratio 1.000 — planar 3-fold symmetric ion): its in-plane
  principal axes are fundamentally ARBITRARY — under sigma = 0.01 A noise
  they swing **88.3 deg (rank) / 44.8 deg (best-match)**.
- **9 of 18 ligands** have an in-plane eigenvalue ratio < 1.15 (marginal).
- The ligand is **static during play** (moved once at start by the
  front-offset; the player never moves it), so a ligand frame degenerates
  to ONE arbitrary per-session basis — and with **helper visuals banned**
  (PROJECT decision: no helper visuals during gameplay), its axes are
  INVISIBLE to the player.
- The useful direction a ligand frame would give — "toward the ligand is
  always Up" — is exactly the EXISTING **Toward-ligand** button.

## 3. Options (transcribed from probes sections 6-7)

Ranking by playability-gain x low-risk x headless-verifiability:
**1 (drop/replace roll) > 6 (Toward-ligand refinement) > 5 (Game-tab
arrows) > 2 (re-align button) > 3 (world-frame) > 4 (ligand-frame).**

### Option A (RECOMMENDED PROPOSAL) — "Straight start": drop the 03-06 start roll + bigger Toward-ligand (candidates 1 + 6)

- **What the human experiences:** every fresh session opens with screen
  axes EXACTLY matching the world/grid axes (screen-up = the grid's up on
  screen; Right/Left/Up/Down/In/Out all read intuitively against the
  grid; the ligand still floats visibly IN FRONT of the grid — eyes ->
  ligand -> grid — via the unchanged front-offset). Toward-ligand becomes
  a real transit tool.
- **What changes technically:** `gamestart.compose_molecule_view` skips
  `_frame_ligand_above_grid`; optionally replaces the roll with a
  geometry-side screen-up ligand translate (same frozen baked-translate
  primitive); a Toward-step constant change or a repeat-acceleration
  counter in the wizard; docstring updates in gamestart steps 5-7.
- **What stays green:** py_compile; purity gates; SMOKE-01/02/03/04/05/06/
  07 (except its PART B step_to_ligand 1.0 A check if the step changes),
  09-14, 17-22; engine/detector/level_spec/persistence byte-identical
  (no DETECTOR_VERSION/FORMAT_VERSION events).
- **What evolves deliberately:** SMOKE-08's two compose asserts (lines
  322-327) replaced by P2-derived pins (post-compose R == identity within
  1e-6; cam_z ligand-vs-grid >= 5.0; cam_x alignment; start detect == 0);
  SMOKE-07's step_to_ligand magnitude check evolves for a new constant;
  the movement-header help line in wizard_text may optionally gain the
  "keys move in your current view direction" phrasing NOW.
- **Frozen decisions re-opened (requires a dated human amendment —
  ROADMAP criterion 2 exists exactly for this):** the 03-06 camera-roll
  composition + the "ligand composes above the grid" clause of the 03-07
  framing law. The "camera-field-surgery-avoided" preference is NOT
  touched — the roll is REMOVED, not replaced by camera surgery.

**Two sub-variants (Q1):**
- **(a1) plain drop** — front-offset only; the invisible 0.44 A
  above-the-grid sign is gone along with the roll.
- **(a2) drop + geometry-side above-grid ligand lift of k A** — proposed
  k = 3-5 A; a baked world-frame translate along R^T.(0,+k,0) applied
  AFTER the front-offset, recomputed per compose — gives a VISIBLE
  above-grid look. CAVEAT: the detector-neutrality property
  "start pose scores 0" (SMOKE-08/15 pin) must be re-verified headless
  for the chosen k (the +8.2 A depth lead gives margin, but this is a
  check, not an assumption).

### Option B — Option A + Game-tab arrow keys (candidates 1 + 5 + 6)

- **What the human experiences:** everything in A, plus the physical
  up/down/left/right arrow keys nudging while the Game tab has focus
  (up/down finally do something — today they scroll command history and

- **What changes technically:** everything in A + a `keyPressEvent` on
  `GameTab` (aamatch/game_window.py:162) routing Qt arrow key codes into

- **What stays green / evolves:** as A; SMOKE-11/14/16 (tab structure)
  stay green; one new unit-testable mapping table if the codes are
  factored pure.
- **The delivery/focus unknown:** keyboard-focus behavior between the
  Game tab, the PyMOL viewer widget, and the command line on this Windows
  build is **GUI-UNKNOWN** (the 03-RESEARCH :226 soft spot — "a 5-minute
  human observation"). It is adjudicated at the final GUI checkpoint; a
  5-minute live observation can instead be done NOW, at this decision
  step (see Task 2 / the checkpoint pack).

### Option C (minimal) — keep everything (roll stays), only retune Toward-ligand (candidate 6 alone)

- **What the human experiences:** unchanged start view (still rolled
  115-128 deg — the disorientation REMAINS), but transits to the ligand
  get much easier.
- **What changes technically:** Toward step size and/or repeat-press
  acceleration only.
- **What stays green:** everything except SMOKE-07's 1.0 A step_to_ligand
  check (deliberate evolution).
- **Frozen decisions re-opened:** NONE (no camera decision touched).
  Lowest-risk — but the standing complaint stays unfixed: P2 shows the
  roll keeps composing fresh every session, so arrows/buttons still read
  as rotated against the grid.

### Option D (NOT RECOMMENDED, recorded for completeness) — world-frame keys (candidate 3) or ligand-frame steps (candidate 4)

- World-frame keys contradict the frozen 03-07 screen-relative law
  ("SCREEN-RELATIVE BY DESIGN"), degrade under orbit, and are dominated
  by A (which makes screen == world at start anyway).
- Ligand-frame steps are rejected by P3 (invisible axes; guanidinium
  degeneracy; static ligand; Toward-ligand already covers the useful
  direction).
- Either would require the dated [GATE] amendment for negative expected
  gain. Answering with either sets the OUT-OF-SCOPE flag (section 6).

### Candidate-2 note (not in A/B/C by default) — mid-game "re-align view" button

Ranked 4th: restores a world-aligned camera mid-game on demand. It is
camera-field surgery (the 03-07 human law prefers geometry-side), yanks

unnecessary IF the start roll is dropped — only the human can say whether
they also orbit-then-get-lost and want the recovery button. Answering YES
on Q4 triggers a revision (no pre-planned recipe).
